Why customers are modernizing now

2013
🧬

AI has shifted from tools that assist humans to agents that act autonomously across complex business processes. As The Agentic AI Genome makes clear, that shift changes what enterprise information needs to be: governed, curated, and permissioned, not just stored. For most on-premises environments, that gap is widening, not closing.

Where environments start
to slow down

On-premises environments require increasing effort to maintain and evolve. These challenges don't resolve on their own. There is no silver lining to postponement; the gap between your environment and what AI requires widens. The cost of waiting is not neutral — it compounds.

Upgrade cycles are delayed or avoided

High risk, long timelines, and long gap time.

Integrations require ongoing effort

Keeping pace with core systems adds constant complexity.

Governance is inconsistent

Cannot enforce access control in a siloed environment.

Information is difficult to use in AI and automation

Limited structure, access control, or context.

Compliance and security posture is harder to maintain

Staying current requires patching, and reevaluating risk.

The cost of staying put is rising

Support costs, maintenance grows, and the financial case to move is growing too.
