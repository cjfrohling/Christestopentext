Why customers are modernizing now

2013
🧬

AI has shifted from the sideline to the center of everything that organizations do. The disruption is accumulating: companies, competitors, the pace of the market are all using AI to go faster, do more, and gain the edge.

Everything is now generated, created, content-reviewed, not just content. For those in information-heavy fields, their work is undergoing deep change. The blueprint is here.

Where environments start
to slow down

On-premises environments require increasing effort to maintain and evolve. These challenges don’t resolve on their own. There is no silver lining to postponement; the gap between your environment and what AI requires widens. The cost of waiting is not neutral — it compounds.
